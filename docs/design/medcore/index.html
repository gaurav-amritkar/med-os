MedCore sample screens

Static design mockups for MedOS, based on Medcore Control Center Dashboard by Sayem (Dribbble). Open a tile to view the mockup; each has a companion spec under screens/.

01
Dashboard
KPIs, admissions & discharges trend, department load, activity feed, ward status.
 02
Bed & Wards
Live bed map, housekeeping queue, transfers, ward occupancy table.
 03
Emergency Flow
Acuity funnel, door-to-triage/doctor timers, ambulance bay, bottleneck feed.
 04
Staff Management
Headcount by role, 7-day shift coverage, today's roster, leave gaps.
 05
Critical Alerts
Alert feed with ack/escalate, SLA trend, escalation matrix.
 06
Capacity Forecasts
The Dribbble screen, recreated: bed occupancy, ICU demand, oxygen & equipment, staff demand.
 07
Operational Reports
Report library, scheduled generation, run history.
 08
Help & Supports
Search, FAQ, documentation links, support contact.
 09
Settings
Profile, hospital, notifications, forecast thresholds, security.
