Frequently asked
How do I assign a bed to an incoming patient?−
Open Bed & Wards, filter to the ward, click a green Available cell, then choose Assign patient. The bed moves to Occupied and writes an audit entry with your identity.
What do the forecast threshold lines mean?+
Green = normal capacity, orange = warning band, red = critical. Thresholds are configured per hospital in Settings → Hospitals and applied to Bed Occupancy, ICU Demand and Oxygen forecasts.
Why can't I acknowledge a critical alert?+
Only roles in the alert's escalation path can acknowledge. If the role is right but the button is disabled, the alert is already claimed by someone else — the trail shows who.
How is patient data encrypted?+
Every identity field (name, contact, IDs) is encrypted per hospital with a tenant data-encryption key. Decryption requires your logged-in session and is always written to the audit trail.
Who do I call for a lab panic value?+
The alert feed escalates automatically: floor → in-charge → HOD → medical superintendent. You never need to call manually — but Ext. 4021 reaches the lab console directly.
